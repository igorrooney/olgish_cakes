# Homepage release-readiness exception

## Status

Project-policy performance exception revalidated on 25 August 2026. The
final production build passes the functional, security, accessibility, SEO and
layout-stability gates, but its measured mobile lab LCP remains above the normal
2.5-second target.

This exception covers homepage mobile lab LCP only. It does not remove the
separate legal and operational release gate in `docs/PRODUCTION_DEPLOYMENT.md`.

## Audit setup

- Page: `/`
- Build: local Next.js 16.2.11 production build
- Lighthouse: 12.8.2, default mobile configuration
- Warm-up: 1 run
- Measurements: 3 clean Lighthouse runs
- Consent UI: shown immediately; optional cookies and scripts were not
  pre-authorised
- Final report date: 25 August 2026
- Reports:
  - `reports/generated/lighthouse-home-2026-08-25-run-1.json`
  - `reports/generated/lighthouse-home-2026-08-25-run-2.json`

## Decision and trade-off

The final audit does not meet the 2.5-second mobile lab LCP target. The exception
is permitted under the project's release policy because:

- the immediate cookie-consent banner is a release requirement and has not
  been deferred or hidden;
- the approved homepage design and hero assets have been preserved;
- invalid homepage structured data and unused review-statistics work were
  removed;
- the centre image is eager-loaded with high fetch priority in AVIF format and
  correctly identified by Lighthouse as the LCP image;
- the `experimental.inlineCss` A/B check was completed and the measured
  first-load result favoured keeping it enabled;
- the production build, full Jest suite, configured coverage gates, type
  checks, lint, schema validation, dependency audit and production-browser
  checks passed after the final fixes;
- Lighthouse accessibility, Best Practices and SEO are 100, and median CLS is
  comfortably below 0.1;
- further material LCP work is likely to require a visible hero or
  consent-banner design change that has not been approved.

## Post-release monitoring

- Monitor the homepage mobile LCP field p75 in production.
- Reopen design work if field p75 remains above 2.5 seconds.
- Re-run Lighthouse against the deployed production URL; local lab results do
  not replace production monitoring.
- Keep the release blocked until the legal and operational owners complete
  every unchecked item in `docs/PRODUCTION_DEPLOYMENT.md`, including processor
  agreements/transfers, ICO fee or exemption, retention ownership, physical
  allergen labelling, testimonial evidence and final UK-qualified-solicitor
  approval.
